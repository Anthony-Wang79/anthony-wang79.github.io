I am a Ph.D. candidate in Economics at Fudan University. My research lies at the intersection of insurance economics, health economics, and household finance. I study how insurance institutions and information shape households’ responses to health risks.

## Research

- **The Impact of Public-Private Partnership Health Plans on Private Insurance**, with [Hui Ding](https://dh-huiding.github.io/) and [Xian Xu](http://xianxu-econ.com/). *Journal of Development Economics*. [View paper](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jdeveco.2026.103780).
